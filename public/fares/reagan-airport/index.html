    DC Metro fare from Reagan National Airport

    5. From Reagan National

    Reagan National (DCA) has its own Metro station a short ride south of the city centre on the Blue and Yellow lines, which makes it the cheap airport transfer in the Washington region. A weekday ride into downtown is a short distance-based fare, well below the $6.75 the longest trips cost. After 9:30 PM and all weekend, every trip from DCA is capped at $2.50.

    Weekday, to downtown -- Opening to 9:30 PM, priced by distance. Live from the WMATA API.
    Night & weekend $2.50 After 9:30 PM, Saturday, Sunday and federal holidays.

    Figures are for Reagan National to Metro Center. Your exact fare depends on where you get off, so check the table below or the calculator.

    When is the fare from Reagan National cheapest?

    Because DCA trips are already short, the saving from riding late is smaller here than from Dulles, and a very short hop can cost the same at any hour. The cap still applies: tap in after 9:30 PM, or on any weekend or federal holiday, and the trip cannot cost more than $2.50.
