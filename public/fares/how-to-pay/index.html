    How to pay for the DC Metro

    Tap a Smart Trip card or a contactless card or phone at the faregate. On Metrorail you must tap the same card in and out, because the exit tap is what sets your fare. On Metrobus you tap once, as you board.

    How do you ride the Metro, start to finish?

    1. 01

      Get a card

      Buy a Smart Trip card for $2.00 at any station fare machine, or just use a contactless card or phone you already carry.

    2. 02

      Tap in

      Hold the card flat on the faregate target. The gate opens, but the fare is not charged yet.

    3. 03

      Ride

      Take your train. Changing between Metrorail lines is part of the same trip and costs nothing extra.

    4. 04

      Tap out, same card

      Tap the exact same card to leave. The distance between your two stations sets the fare, so skipping this step costs you money.

    Metrobus works differently: one tap as you board, no tap on the way off. Rail to bus and bus to rail transfers carry a $2.25 discount within two hours when you use a Smart Trip card.

    Smart Trip or contactless: which should you use?

    Smart Trip

    $2.00 for the card, plus the value you load
    • Physical card or a card in your phone wallet
    • Required for passes and reduced fares
    • Reload with cash at any station machine
    • Cards printed "SmarTrip" still work; the name changed, the card did not

    Contactless

    Same fare, no card fee
    • Any Visa, Mastercard, American Express or Discover card
    • Apple Pay and Google Pay both work at the gate
    • Best for visitors and one-off trips
    • Cannot hold a pass, so it is pay-per-ride only

    Contactless and Smart Trip are charged the identical fare. The only real difference is that passes, reduced fares and employer transit benefits need a Smart Trip card.

    Questions people ask

    Do you tap out on the DC Metro?

    Yes, on Metrorail. The exit tap is what calculates your distance-based fare. On Metrobus you tap once, when you board.

    What happens if you forget to tap out?

    The system keeps the maximum fare it held at entry, so you can pay more than the trip was worth. Always leave with the same card you entered with.

    Can two people share one card?

    No. Every rider needs their own tap in and tap out. The exception is children: up to two children age 4 and under ride free with each fare-paying adult.

    Does the Metro take cash?

    Not at the rail faregates. Load cash onto a card at a station fare machine instead. Metrobus does accept exact-change cash on board.

    What if your balance is too low to exit?

    Add value at an Exitfare machine, which sits near the faregates on the paid side, then tap out as normal.

    Is Smart Trip cheaper than tapping a credit card?

    No. Both are charged the same fare. Smart Trip only wins when you need a pass, a reduced fare, or employer transit benefits, and it saves you the $2.00 card fee if you already have a contactless card.

    Know your fare before you go

    Check what any trip costs, including the late night and weekend price and the reduced fare, before you tap in.
