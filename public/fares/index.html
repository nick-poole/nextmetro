    Fare Structure

    Metrorail uses a distance-based fare system. The further you travel, the more you pay. A weekday fare (opening – 9:30 PM) is $2.25 for the first three composite miles and $0.45 for each mile after that, up to $6.75. Late night and weekend fares are charged the same way but capped at $2.50, which a trip reaches after about three and a half miles. There is no separate rush-hour surcharge.

    Fare Category Weekday Late Night / Weekend Senior/Disabled & Metro Lift
    Boarding (first 3 composite miles) $2.25 $2.25 $1.10
    Each composite mile beyond 3 + $0.45 + $0.45 to the cap 50% of the fare
    Medium trip (3–6 composite miles) $2.25 – $3.60 $2.25 – $2.50 $1.10 – $1.80
    Long trip (6–13 composite miles) $3.60 – $6.75 $2.50 $1.80 – $3.35
    Maximum fare (13+ composite miles) $6.75 $2.50 $3.35

    A composite mile is Metro's own unit: the average of the distance the train travels between your two stations and the straight-line distance between them. It is usually a little shorter than the route on a map, which is why a fare can come in below what the mileage suggests. Reduced fares are half the applicable fare, rounded down to the next $0.05 — so the $1.10 minimum and $3.35 maximum are simply half of $2.25 and $6.75. Late night and weekend reduced fares run $1.10 – $1.25.

    Fare Hours

    Weekday fares are distance-based. Late night (after 9:30 PM) and weekend fares use a reduced flat rate. Federal holidays use weekend fare rates.

    When Fare Rates Apply
    Weekdays (opening–9:30 PM) $2.25 – $6.75 (distance-based)
    Late Night (after 9:30 PM) $2.25 – $2.50
    Weekends & Holidays $2.25 – $2.50

    For full operating hours, train frequency, and holiday schedules, see Metro hours & schedule.

    Passes

    Metro offers several pass options for frequent riders. Passes cover unlimited Metrorail and Metrobus rides for the duration of the pass.

    Pass Price Details
    1-Day Unlimited $13.50 Unlimited rail & bus; expires end of operating day
    3-Day Unlimited $33.75 Unlimited rail & bus; 3 consecutive operating days
    7-Day Unlimited $60.75 Unlimited rail & bus; 7 consecutive operating days
    7-Day Short-Trip $40.50 Rail trips up to $4.50; excess deducted from stored value
    7-Day Regional Bus $13.50 Metrobus & regional partners; $6.75 reduced fare
    Monthly Unlimited $72 – $216 Priced at 32× your fare level; includes unlimited bus

    Passes require a SmarTrip® card — they cannot be used with contactless tap-to-pay. For full pass details, see WMATA pass options.

    Transfers & SmarTrip

    Transfer Rules

    • Rail-to-rail transfers are included in your fare during a continuous trip
    • Rail-to-bus and bus-to-rail transfers are free ($2.25 discount) within 2 hours with SmarTrip
    • Bus-to-bus transfers are free with unlimited transfers within 2 hours
    • Transfers are valid for up to 2 hours from the start of your trip

    SmarTrip & How to Pay

    A SmarTrip card or contactless payment is required to ride Metrorail. Cards cost $2.00 and are available at fare machines in every station.

    You can also tap a contactless credit/debit card or mobile wallet (Apple Pay, Google Pay) at fare gates.
