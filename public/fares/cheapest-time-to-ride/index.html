    The cheapest time to ride the DC Metro

    1. Home
    2. /
    3. Fares
    4. /
    5. Cheapest Time to Ride

    The cheapest time to ride is after 9:30 PM on weekdays, at any hour on Saturday and Sunday, and on federal holidays. Every trip is capped at $2.25 to $2.50 in those windows, however far you travel, against a weekday range of $2.25 to $6.75.

    Why does the time of day change your fare?

    Weekday daytime fares are distance-based: $2.25 covers the first three composite miles, then each further composite mile adds $0.45, up to a maximum of $6.75. Once the clock passes 9:30 PM, the whole system switches to a flat cap. Weekends and federal holidays run at that cap all day. There is no rush-hour surcharge: weekday daytime travel is simply priced by distance.

    Weekday, before 9:30 PM Distance-based

    $2.25longer trip$6.75

    After 9:30 PM, weekends, holidays Flat cap

    $2.25any distance$2.50

    Save up to $4.25 on a long trip

    When does the cheap fare apply?

    Metrorail fare by time of day and day of week
    When Fare
    Weekdays, after 9:30 PM $2.25 to $2.50
    Saturday, all day $2.25 to $2.50
    Sunday, all day $2.25 to $2.50
    Federal holidays, all day $2.25 to $2.50
    Weekdays, opening to 9:30 PM $2.25 to $6.75

    The capped fare reaches $2.50 after about three and a half composite miles, so most trips outside the immediate centre pay the full $2.50. For first and last train times, see Metro hours and schedule.

    Does the 9:30 PM cutoff use your tap in or tap out?

    It uses your tap in. Enter the system at 9:28 PM and the weekday distance fare applies to the whole trip, even if you exit at 10:15 PM. Wait until 9:31 PM to tap in and you get the flat cap. This is the single detail that catches people out, and on a long trip it is worth over four dollars.

    What does it look like on a real trip?

    Example trips comparing the weekday fare with the capped fare
    Trip Weekday Night / weekend You save
    Dulles Airport to Metro Center $6.75 $2.50 $4.25
    Shady Grove to Glenmont $6.75 $2.50 $4.25
    Dupont Circle to Gallery Pl-Chinatown $2.25 $2.25 $0.00

    Short trips are already at the $2.25 floor, so waiting for the cap saves nothing there. The saving grows with distance, which is why airport and end-of-line trips benefit most.
