    DC Metro fare from Dulles Airport

    1. Home
    2. /

    4. /
    5. From Dulles Airport

    A weekday Metro trip from Washington Dulles (IAD) to downtown DC costs $6.75, the highest fare Metrorail charges, because Dulles sits near the western end of the Silver Line. Tap in after 9:30 PM, or any time on a weekend or federal holiday, and the same trip is capped at $2.50.

    Weekday, to downtown $6.75 Opening to 9:30 PM. This is the distance maximum.
    Night & weekend $2.50 After 9:30 PM, Saturday, Sunday and federal holidays.

    Figures are for Dulles to Metro Center and are pulled from the WMATA API. Your exact fare depends on where you get off.

    When is the fare from Dulles cheapest?

    A trip from Dulles is the strongest case on the whole system for riding late. You go from the $6.75 maximum down to the $2.50 cap, a $4.25 saving on exactly the same ride. The cutoff is measured when you tap in, so entering at 9:28 PM charges the weekday fare even if you exit at 10:15.

    Good to know before you tap in

    Line

    Silver Line, toward Downtown Largo

    Station

    Washington Dulles International Airport, walkway from the main terminal

    Payment

    Smart Trip card or contactless tap, including Apple Pay and Google Pay. How to pay

    Children

    Up to two children age 4 and under ride free with each fare-paying adult

    Older cards still printed with the "SmarTrip" spelling work exactly the same as the rebranded Smart Trip cards.
