    Is a monthly Metro pass worth it?

    1. Home
    2. /
    3. Fares
    4. /
    5. Monthly Pass

    A Monthly Unlimited pass is priced at 32 times your one-way fare, so it pays for itself once you take more than 32 one-way trips in a month, which is about 16 round-trip commuting days. Commute four or more days a week and the pass wins.

    Where is the break-even point?

    Pay-per-ride cost climbs with every trip you take. A pass is a flat price. Where the two lines cross is your break-even point, and after that every extra ride is effectively free.

    What do the passes cost?

    Metro pass prices and break-even points
    Pass Price Break-even
    1-Day Unlimited $13.50 About 4 trips in one day
    3-Day Unlimited $33.75 About 10 trips over 3 days
    7-Day Unlimited $60.75 About 18 trips in a week
    7-Day Short-Trip $40.50 Rail trips up to $4.50; the excess comes off stored value
    7-Day Regional Bus $13.50 Metrobus and regional partners only
    Monthly Unlimited $72 to $216 32 one-way trips per month

    Every pass includes unlimited Metrobus. Break-even figures for the fixed-price passes assume the $3.35 average rail fare; at the $2.25 minimum you need more trips, and at the $6.75 maximum you need fewer. Full pass details sit on the fares hub.

    When is a monthly pass not worth it?

    If you work hybrid, ride a few days a week, or take mostly short trips already close to the $2.25 floor, pay-per-ride usually wins. Two other things to weigh: passes need a Smart Trip card and do not work with contactless tap-to-pay, and a monthly pass does not stretch further just because you travel late, since off-peak trips are already capped at $2.50.
